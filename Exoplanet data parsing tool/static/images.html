Transit finder FITS → light curve

FITS → light curve

Turn raw science images and matching dark frames into a time–flux table. Process one field, filter and camera setup at a time.

1. Load the images

Darks must match exposure and camera settings. No exposure scaling or flat-field correction is applied. Already dark-corrected images are rejected when identified by their headers.

Up to 10,000 science images and 128 darks; 512 MiB per image and 20 GiB of staged uploads. Source images stay unchanged; finished tables are saved under Desktop/Current/Exoplanet Transit Finder Stuff/Image Light Curves.

2. Pick the stars

Choose isolated stars. One target is required; up to eight comparison stars are optional. Comparison stars should be stable and unsaturated.

  1. No stars selected.

Tracking searches near the selected positions. Split meridian flips, large rotations and moves into separate runs.

3. Make the table

DATE-AVG is treated as a midpoint. The output records timing assumptions; missing time scales remain unverified and no barycentric correction is made.
